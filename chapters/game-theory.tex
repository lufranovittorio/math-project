\chapter{Game Theory}\label{game-theory}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Mathematical models of strategic interaction: equilibria, their existence, and the design of games.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{convex-analysis} (Convex Analysis), Section~\ref{convex-analysis:section-linear-programming} (Linear Programming) and Chapter~\ref{singular-homology} (Singular Homology).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Games in normal form, dominance, Nash equilibria.
\item Zero-sum games: von Neumann's minimax theorem.
\item Existence of Nash equilibria via Brouwer and Kakutani; Sperner's lemma.
\item Extensive games, backward induction, subgame perfection, Zermelo's theorem.
\item Cooperative games: the core, Bondareva--Shapley, the Shapley value.
\item Social choice: Arrow's theorem; auctions and mechanism design.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
