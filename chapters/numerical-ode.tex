\chapter{Numerical Methods for Ordinary Differential Equations}\label{numerical-ode}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Approximating solutions of initial value problems: one-step and multistep methods, their convergence
and stability.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{numerical-analysis} (Numerical Analysis) and Chapter~\ref{ordinary-differential-equations} (Ordinary Differential Equations).

\noindent\textbf{On the way to the Hodge conjecture.} This chapter is not needed on the direct
route; it belongs to the reading path of numerical analysis
(Subsection~\ref{introduction:subsection-reading-paths}).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Euler's method, consistency, stability, convergence.
\item Runge--Kutta methods, order conditions, Butcher tableaux.
\item Linear multistep methods, Dahlquist's theorems.
\item Stiffness, A-stability, implicit methods.
\item Geometric integration: symplectic methods.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
