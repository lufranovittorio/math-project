\chapter{Operator Algebras}\label{operator-algebras}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Algebras of bounded operators on Hilbert spaces: the spectral theorem in full generality, $C^*$-algebras
and von Neumann algebras.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{functional-analysis} (Functional Analysis) and Chapter~\ref{measure-theory} (Measure and Integration).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Banach algebras and the Gelfand transform.
\item $C^*$-algebras: the commutative Gelfand--Naimark theorem, continuous functional calculus, positivity, the GNS construction.
\item The spectral theorem for bounded and unbounded self-adjoint operators.
\item Von Neumann algebras: the bicommutant theorem, projections, factors and their types (introduction).
\item Noncommutative geometry (survey): spectral triples, the noncommutative torus, cyclic cohomology.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
