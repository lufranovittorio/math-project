\chapter{Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves}\label{elliptic-curves-arithmetic}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Rational points on elliptic curves over $\QQ$ and over finite fields.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{curves} (Algebraic Curves and Riemann Surfaces), Chapter~\ref{algebraic-number-theory} (Algebraic Number Theory) and Chapter~\ref{p-adic-numbers} (p-adic Numbers).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Weierstrass equations, the group law, torsion points; the Nagell--Lutz theorem.
\item Elliptic curves over finite fields: Hasse's bound; elliptic curve cryptography and Lenstra's factoring method.
\item The Mordell--Weil theorem: weak Mordell--Weil and heights.
\item The congruent number problem; the conjecture of Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer (statement); modularity (statement).
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
