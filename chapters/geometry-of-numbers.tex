\chapter{Geometry of Numbers}\label{geometry-of-numbers}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Lattices and convex bodies, and the two finiteness theorems of algebraic number theory.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{algebraic-number-theory} (Algebraic Number Theory) and Chapter~\ref{measure-theory} (Measure and Integration).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Lattices in $\RR^n$, covolume; Minkowski's convex body theorem and the theorems on successive minima (statement).
\item Applications: sums of two and four squares again, Legendre's theorem.
\item The Minkowski embedding; the Minkowski bound and the finiteness of the class number; computation of class groups of quadratic fields.
\item Hermite's theorem on fields of bounded discriminant; Minkowski's theorem that $|d_K| > 1$ for $K \neq \QQ$.
\item Dirichlet's unit theorem, regulators; units of real quadratic fields and Pell's equation.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
