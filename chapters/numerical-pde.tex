\chapter{Numerical Methods for Partial Differential Equations}\label{numerical-pde}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Finite differences and finite elements, with their stability and convergence theory.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{numerical-linear-algebra} (Numerical Linear Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{evolution-equations} (Evolution Equations).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Finite differences for elliptic, parabolic and hyperbolic problems; the CFL condition.
\item Stability and the Lax equivalence theorem; von Neumann analysis.
\item Finite elements: Galerkin method, C\'ea's lemma, error estimates.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
