\chapter{Calculus of Variations}\label{calculus-of-variations}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Minimising functionals: the Euler--Lagrange equations, the direct method, and symmetries.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{sobolev-spaces} (Sobolev Spaces) and Chapter~\ref{functional-analysis} (Functional Analysis).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The Euler--Lagrange equations; classical problems: brachistochrone, geodesics, minimal surfaces of revolution.
\item Second variation, Legendre and Jacobi conditions.
\item The direct method: lower semicontinuity, convexity, coercivity; existence of minimisers.
\item Constraints and Lagrange multipliers; isoperimetric problems.
\item Noether's theorem; Hamiltonian formulation.
\item Optimal control: Pontryagin's maximum principle, the Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman equation and dynamic programming, linear-quadratic regulators.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
