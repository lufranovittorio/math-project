\chapter{Nonlinear Optimisation}\label{nonlinear-optimisation}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Optimality conditions and algorithms for smooth and convex optimisation.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{convex-analysis} (Convex Analysis) and Chapter~\ref{numerical-analysis} (Numerical Analysis).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item First- and second-order conditions; Lagrange multipliers and KKT revisited, constraint qualifications.
\item Gradient descent, line search, convergence rates for convex and strongly convex functions.
\item Newton and quasi-Newton methods.
\item Constrained methods: projection, penalties, augmented Lagrangian.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
