\chapter{Algebraic Number Theory}\label{algebraic-number-theory}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Rings of integers of number fields: ideals, class groups and units.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{commutative-algebra} (Commutative Algebra), Chapter~\ref{fields} (Fields and Galois Theory) and Chapter~\ref{number-theory} (Elementary Number Theory).

\noindent\textbf{On the way to the Hodge conjecture.} This chapter is not needed on the direct
route; it belongs to the reading path of number theory
(Subsection~\ref{introduction:subsection-reading-paths}).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Number fields, rings of integers, discriminants.
\item Dedekind domains and unique factorisation of ideals.
\item Minkowski's geometry of numbers; finiteness of the class number; Dirichlet's unit theorem.
\item Decomposition of primes, ramification; quadratic and cyclotomic fields.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
