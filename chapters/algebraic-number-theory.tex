\chapter{Algebraic Number Theory}\label{algebraic-number-theory}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Number fields and their rings of integers; Dedekind domains and the factorisation of ideals.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{fields} (Fields and Galois Theory), Chapter~\ref{commutative-algebra} (Commutative Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{number-theory} (Elementary Number Theory).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Algebraic integers, rings of integers, trace, norm and discriminant; integral bases.
\item Quadratic fields in detail: rings of integers, units, factorisation of primes.
\item Dedekind domains: unique factorisation of ideals, the class group, the Chinese remainder theorem for ideals.
\item Decomposition of primes in extensions: $e$, $f$, $g$ and $\sum e_if_i = n$, Kummer--Dedekind, ramification and the discriminant.
\item Galois extensions: decomposition and inertia groups, Frobenius elements; quadratic reciprocity from the splitting of primes in cyclotomic fields.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
