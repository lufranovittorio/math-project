\section{Algebraic Number Theory}\label{algebraic-number-theory:section-number-fields}
Number fields and their rings of integers; Dedekind domains and the factorisation of ideals.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{fields} (Fields and Galois Theory), Chapter~\ref{commutative-algebra} (Commutative Algebra) and Chapter~\ref{number-theory} (Elementary Number Theory).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Algebraic integers, rings of integers, trace, norm and discriminant; integral bases.
\item Quadratic fields in detail: rings of integers, units, factorisation of primes.
\item Dedekind domains: unique factorisation of ideals, the class group, the Chinese remainder theorem for ideals.
\item Decomposition of primes in extensions: $e$, $f$, $g$ and $\sum e_if_i = n$, Kummer--Dedekind, ramification and the discriminant.
\item Galois extensions: decomposition and inertia groups, Frobenius elements; quadratic reciprocity from the splitting of primes in cyclotomic fields.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Class Groups and Units}\label{algebraic-number-theory:section-class-groups-and-units}
The two finiteness theorems of algebraic number theory, proved with the geometry of numbers: the class group of a number field is finite, and its unit group is finitely generated.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Section~\ref{algebraic-number-theory:section-number-fields}, Chapter~\ref{geometry-of-numbers} (Geometry of Numbers) and Chapter~\ref{continued-fractions} (Continued Fractions and Diophantine Approximation).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The Minkowski embedding of a number field; the ring of integers and its ideals as lattices, and their covolumes.
\item The Minkowski bound; finiteness of the class number; computation of class groups of quadratic and cubic fields.
\item Minkowski's theorem that $|d_K| > 1$ for $K \neq \QQ$; Hermite's theorem on fields of bounded discriminant.
\item Dirichlet's unit theorem and the regulator; units of real quadratic fields and the Pell equation; units of cubic fields.
\item The Dedekind zeta function and the analytic class number formula.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Cyclotomic Fields}\label{algebraic-number-theory:section-cyclotomic-fields}
The fields generated by roots of unity, and Kummer's work on Fermat's last theorem.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Section~\ref{algebraic-number-theory:section-class-groups-and-units} (Class Groups and Units).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Cyclotomic polynomials and fields; the ring of integers $\ZZ[\zeta_n]$, discriminant, splitting of primes.
\item Gauss sums, quadratic subfields, the Kronecker--Weber theorem (statement here, proof in Chapter~\ref{local-fields}).
\item Regular primes, Kummer's lemma on units, and Fermat's last theorem for regular primes; Kummer's criterion via Bernoulli numbers (statement).
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
