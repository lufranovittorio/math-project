\chapter{Classical Geometry}\label{classical-geometry}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The geometry of Euclid and its descendants, treated through the groups that act on each space, as in Klein's Erlangen programme: Euclidean, affine, projective, inversive, spherical and hyperbolic
geometry, with the classical theorems a curious reader looks for (triangle centres, Ptolemy, Desargues, Pappus, Pascal, Brianchon, Feuerbach, Morley) and their proofs.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{linear-algebra} (Linear Algebra), Chapter~\ref{groups} (Groups) and Chapter~\ref{fields} (Fields and Galois Theory).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Axiomatics: Hilbert's axioms, the parallel postulate and its equivalents, models; coordinates from axioms (fields from Desargues and Pappus).
\item The Euclidean plane and space: isometries and their classification (reflections generate), similarities; finite subgroups of $\mathrm{O}(2)$ and $\mathrm{O}(3)$ and the Platonic solids.
\item Triangle geometry: centres (centroid, circumcentre, incentre, orthocentre), Euler line, nine-point circle and Feuerbach's theorem, Ceva and Menelaus, Stewart, Ptolemy, Morley's theorem, Heron's
formula, Napoleon's theorem, the Fermat point; trigonometry (laws of sines and cosines).
\item Circles and inversion: power of a point, radical axis, inversion and its properties, Apollonius' problem, Steiner chains, Möbius transformations and the inversive plane.
\item Affine geometry: affine spaces and maps, barycentric coordinates, affine classification of conics and quadrics.
\item Projective geometry: projective spaces and transformations, homogeneous coordinates, duality, Desargues and Pappus, the cross-ratio, the fundamental theorem of projective geometry, conics
(Pascal, Brianchon, pole and polar), finite projective planes.
\item Spherical geometry: spherical triangles, Girard's theorem on the area, spherical trigonometry.
\item Hyperbolic geometry: the hyperboloid, Poincaré disc, upper half plane and Klein models; isometries ($\mathrm{PSL}_2(\RR)$), geodesics, area of triangles, tilings, hyperbolic trigonometry.
\item Ruler-and-compass constructions revisited (Mohr--Mascheroni, Poncelet--Steiner, origami), with pointers to Chapter~\ref{fields}.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
