\chapter{The Probabilistic Method}\label{probabilistic-method}
\chapterstatus{planned}

To prove that an object with some property exists, show that a random object has the property with
positive probability. This chapter introduces finite probability spaces and develops the method of
Erd\H{o}s.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{combinatorics} (Combinatorics) and Chapter~\ref{graph-theory} (Graph Theory).

\noindent\textbf{On the way to the Hodge conjecture.} This chapter is not needed on the direct
route; it belongs to the reading path of discrete mathematics
(Subsection~\ref{introduction:subsection-reading-paths}).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Finite probability spaces, expectation, linearity of expectation.
\item First applications: lower bounds for Ramsey numbers, tournaments, sum-free sets.
\item Alterations, the second moment method, threshold functions in random graphs.
\item The Lov\'asz local lemma and its applications.
\item Concentration: Chernoff bounds, martingales on finite spaces and Azuma's inequality.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
