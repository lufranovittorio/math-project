\chapter{Modular Forms}\label{modular-forms}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Holomorphic functions on the upper half-plane with a symmetry under $\SL_2(\ZZ)$.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{complex-analysis} (Complex Analysis), Chapter~\ref{number-theory} (Elementary Number Theory) and Chapter~\ref{curves} (Algebraic Curves and Riemann Surfaces).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The modular group, its fundamental domain; modular forms and cusp forms.
\item Eisenstein series, the discriminant, the $j$-function; dimension formulas.
\item Hecke operators; $L$-functions of modular forms.
\item Theta functions and sums of squares revisited.
\item The Langlands programme (survey): automorphic representations, Galois representations attached to modular forms, reciprocity and functoriality, the Sato--Tate conjecture (Taylor et al., statement).
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
