\section{Sobolev Spaces}\label{sobolev-spaces:section-sobolev-spaces}
The function spaces in which partial differential equations are solved, on domains of $\RR^n$,
extending Section~\ref{distributions:section-sobolev}.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{distributions} (Distributions and Sobolev Spaces), Chapter~\ref{measure-theory} (Measure and Integration) and Chapter~\ref{functional-analysis} (Functional Analysis).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Weak derivatives; $W^{k,p}(\Omega)$; approximation by smooth functions (Meyers--Serrin).
\item Extension operators for Lipschitz domains; traces on the boundary.
\item Embedding theorems: Gagliardo--Nirenberg--Sobolev, Morrey; the Rellich--Kondrachov compactness theorem.
\item Poincar\'e inequalities; the spaces $W^{k,p}_0$ and $H^{-1}$.
\item Fractional Sobolev spaces (introduction).
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Elliptic Partial Differential Equations}\label{sobolev-spaces:section-elliptic-pde}
Laplace's equation and second-order elliptic equations: classical theory, weak solutions and
regularity.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Section~\ref{sobolev-spaces:section-sobolev-spaces}.

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Harmonic functions: mean value property, maximum principle, Harnack, Liouville; the Poisson kernel.
\item The Dirichlet problem: Perron's method, barriers; Green's functions.
\item Weak solutions: the Lax--Milgram theorem, existence for divergence-form equations.
\item Interior and boundary regularity in $H^k$; Schauder estimates (statement).
\item Maximum principles for non-divergence equations; eigenvalues of the Laplacian.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Evolution Equations}\label{sobolev-spaces:section-evolution-equations}
The heat, wave and transport equations, and the abstract theory of evolution: semigroups and energy
methods.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Section~\ref{sobolev-spaces:section-elliptic-pde} (Elliptic Partial Differential Equations).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The heat equation: fundamental solution, maximum principle, uniqueness and smoothing.
\item The wave equation: d'Alembert, spherical means, Kirchhoff, finite speed of propagation, energy.
\item First-order equations: characteristics, conservation laws, shocks, entropy solutions.
\item Galerkin methods for parabolic and hyperbolic equations.
\item Semigroups of operators: Hille--Yosida, Lumer--Phillips.
\item Symmetric hyperbolic systems.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
