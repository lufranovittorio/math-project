\chapter{Evolution Equations}\label{evolution-equations}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The heat, wave and transport equations, and the abstract theory of evolution: semigroups and energy
methods.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{elliptic-pde} (Elliptic Partial Differential Equations).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The heat equation: fundamental solution, maximum principle, uniqueness and smoothing.
\item The wave equation: d'Alembert, spherical means, Kirchhoff, finite speed of propagation, energy.
\item First-order equations: characteristics, conservation laws, shocks, entropy solutions.
\item Galerkin methods for parabolic and hyperbolic equations.
\item Semigroups of operators: Hille--Yosida, Lumer--Phillips.
\item Symmetric hyperbolic systems.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
