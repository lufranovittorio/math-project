\chapter{Convex and Discrete Geometry}\label{discrete-geometry}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Convex sets and polytopes in $\RR^n$ and the combinatorics of finite configurations of points, lines and spheres: Euler's formula, regular polytopes, Helly-type theorems, packings and tilings, the
Brunn--Minkowski inequality, and lattice-point counting in polytopes.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{linear-algebra} (Linear Algebra), Chapter~\ref{combinatorics} (Combinatorics), Chapter~\ref{graph-theory} (Graph Theory) and Chapter~\ref{classical-geometry}
(Classical Geometry).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Convex sets: Carathéodory, Radon and Helly theorems, separation, extreme points (Minkowski's theorem in finite dimension), the centre-point theorem, Tverberg (statement).
\item Polytopes: $V$- and $H$-descriptions (Minkowski--Weyl), faces, the face lattice, polarity; Euler's formula and the Euler--Poincaré relation, the Dehn--Sommerville equations, cyclic polytopes and the
upper bound theorem (statement), Steinitz's theorem (statement).
\item Regular polytopes: the Platonic solids, Schläfli symbols, the six regular $4$-polytopes, the three families in higher dimensions; Cauchy's rigidity theorem; Hilbert's third problem and the Dehn
invariant.
\item Mixed volumes and inequalities: Steiner's formula, the Brunn--Minkowski inequality, the isoperimetric inequality, Minkowski's mixed volumes (survey).
\item Lattice polytopes: Pick's theorem revisited, Ehrhart polynomials and reciprocity.
\item Incidence geometry: Sylvester--Gallai, the Szemerédi--Trotter theorem (crossing-number proof), Erdős distance problems.
\item Packings, coverings and tilings: Kepler's conjecture (Hales, statement), kissing numbers, periodic and aperiodic tilings (Penrose), Voronoi and Delaunay decompositions.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
