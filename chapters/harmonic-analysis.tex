\chapter{Harmonic Analysis}\label{harmonic-analysis}
\chapterstatus{planned}

Fourier series and integrals in depth: convergence, the $L^p$ theory, singular integrals, and harmonic
analysis on locally compact abelian groups.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{measure-theory} (Measure and Integration), Chapter~\ref{functional-analysis} (Functional Analysis) and Chapter~\ref{distributions} (Distributions and Sobolev Spaces).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Fourier series: Dirichlet and Fej\'er kernels, pointwise convergence, Gibbs phenomenon, Parseval.
\item The Hardy--Littlewood maximal function; interpolation (Riesz--Thorin, Marcinkiewicz).
\item The Hilbert transform and Calder\'on--Zygmund singular integrals.
\item Littlewood--Paley theory and multipliers.
\item Locally compact abelian groups, Pontryagin duality, Plancherel; the Peter--Weyl theorem for compact groups.
\item Representations of non-compact groups (survey): unitary representations of $\mathrm{SL}_2(\RR)$, the Plancherel theorem for $\mathrm{SL}_2(\RR)$, automorphic forms as functions on $\Gamma\backslash G$.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
