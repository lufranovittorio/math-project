\chapter{Dynamical Systems}\label{dynamical-systems}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The long-time behaviour of flows and iterated maps: limit sets, hyperbolicity, bifurcations and chaos.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{ordinary-differential-equations} (Ordinary Differential Equations).

\noindent\textbf{On the way to the Hodge conjecture.} This chapter is not needed on the direct
route; it belongs to the reading path of differential equations
(Subsection~\ref{introduction:subsection-reading-paths}).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Flows and maps, orbits, limit sets; the Poincar\'e--Bendixson theorem.
\item Hyperbolic fixed points: Hartman--Grobman; stable and unstable manifolds.
\item Periodic orbits and Poincar\'e maps; structural stability.
\item Bifurcations: saddle-node, pitchfork, Hopf.
\item One-dimensional dynamics: the logistic map, period doubling, Sharkovskii's theorem.
\item Chaos: symbolic dynamics, the horseshoe, Lyapunov exponents.
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
