\chapter{Mirror Symmetry}\label{mirror-symmetry}
\chapterstatus{planned}

The duality, discovered in string theory, between the symplectic geometry of a Calabi--Yau manifold and the complex geometry of a mirror manifold: enumerative predictions, the
Strominger--Yau--Zaslow picture of mirror pairs as dual special Lagrangian torus fibrations, and Kontsevich's homological mirror symmetry.

\noindent\textbf{Prerequisites.} Chapter~\ref{calabi-yau} (Calabi--Yau Manifolds), Chapter~\ref{symplectic-geometry} (Symplectic Geometry), Chapter~\ref{intersection-theory} (Algebraic Cycles and Chow
Groups), Chapter~\ref{vhs} (Variations of Hodge Structure) and Chapter~\ref{quantum-mechanics} (Quantum Mechanics).

\noindent\textbf{Planned contents.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The Hodge-theoretic mirror: mirror pairs of Calabi--Yau threefolds, $h^{1,1} \leftrightarrow h^{2,1}$; Batyrev's construction from reflexive polytopes (toric varieties, survey); the quintic and its
mirror.
\item The A-model: $J$-holomorphic curves, Gromov compactness (survey), Gromov--Witten invariants, quantum cohomology of $\mathbb{P}^n$, Kontsevich's formula for rational plane curves.
\item The B-model: periods, the Picard--Fuchs equation of the mirror quintic, the mirror map, the Yukawa coupling; the Candelas--de la Ossa--Green--Parkes prediction ($2875$ lines, $609250$ conics) and
Givental's and Lian--Liu--Yau's theorems (statement).
\item SYZ: special Lagrangian submanifolds (calibrations, McLean's deformation theorem), special Lagrangian torus fibrations, the Strominger--Yau--Zaslow conjecture, T-duality of torus fibres,
semi-flat mirrors, singular fibres and the Gross--Siebert programme (survey); elliptic curves and K3 surfaces as worked examples.
\item Homological mirror symmetry: derived categories of coherent sheaves (Fourier--Mukai transforms, Bondal--Orlov), Fukaya categories (survey), Kontsevich's conjecture and the elliptic curve
(Polishchuk--Zaslow).
\item Topological quantum field theories (Atiyah's axioms) as the framework of the A- and B-models (survey).
\item Exercises with solutions.
\end{enumerate}
